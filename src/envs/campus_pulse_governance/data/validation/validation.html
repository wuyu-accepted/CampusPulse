哪些结论有数据支撑

主张状态原因补齐证据
Conditional within-event normalized forum post arrival shape已做多事件留一回测Daily proxy-share arrival shape is tested with deterministic separated events and leave-one-event-out folds for both focus scenarios.Validate keyword event definitions and map synthetic decision rounds to an operational service cadence before forecasting absolute workload.
Event occurrence and absolute forum post volume现有数据不支持Conditioning on observed peaks cannot test when an event will occur or its absolute workload.Declare an operational time unit and prospectively test occurrence and count forecasts.
Probability that a thread receives any comment已做时间外检验Final linked-comment presence is observable and is tested on a later holdout window.Add human-reviewed need labels to replace overlapping keyword proxies.
Cumulative thread comment-count distribution已做时间外检验Final linked-comment counts are observable; binned CDF and positive-count mean are checked.Add unique responder counts if the source system can expose them safely.
Urgency mix and high-risk identification现有数据不支持No human-reviewed urgency or risk labels exist in the supplied data.Create a double-annotated, adjudicated urgency sample.
Real response speed or information diffusion现有数据不支持Linked comment real_date inherits the parent post time and cannot measure response latency.Collect genuine comment creation timestamps and reply-to structure.
FAQ, peer, routing, and capacity intervention effects现有数据不支持The supplied forum data contain no intervention assignment or operational outcome logs.Run a staged pilot with intervention logs and pre-registered operational outcomes.
Institutional resolution, cost, and service capacity现有数据不支持No service ticket completion, staffing, or cost records are linked to forum posts.Obtain de-identified service ticket and staffing aggregates.
Demographic fairness or population impact现有数据不支持No persistent identity or demographic attributes exist, by design.Use consented aggregate survey or administrative statistics with privacy review.

限制

  • Keyword proxy categories overlap and are not human-reviewed ground truth.
  • The validation window is restricted to one stable post-discontinuity collection regime.
  • Final comment counts measure cumulative attention, not timeliness, unique responders, or solution quality.
  • Policy-effect outputs remain assumption-driven until intervention and service outcome logs exist.
  • Event peaks are discovered from observed keyword-proxy activity; backtests are conditional on an event and do not forecast event occurrence or absolute workload.
  • Annotation completeness does not establish label validity; sentiment, tags, analysis, and enriched text are not used for current scoring.

评分规则使用事先声明的实用误差阈值，而不是利用大样本制造显著性。关键词类别可以重叠，且仍需人工标注集验证。
